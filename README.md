I started asking biological questions to move **beyond artificial tasks toward real ones** — tasks whose rules were written by nature rather than by a benchmark designer, and are still waiting to be found. Finding those rules is what excites me. My work follows biological information from genomes to cells, proteins and drugs: layer-wise interpretability of DNA language models, zero-shot perturbation prediction, protein-function prediction, phenotype-guided drug discovery and a genomics tool-use agent.
